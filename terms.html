Money Coach — SMS Terms & Conditions

Effective date: August 2, 2026

1. About this program

Money Coach is a private, invitation-only SMS service operated by an individual to send members of their own household short notifications about spending and to answer those members' questions about their own budget and balance. It is not offered to the public. It is not a lender, debt collector, credit-repair service, or financial institution, and it sends no marketing or promotional messages.

2. How you opt in (consent)

There is no public sign-up form. Recipients are members of the operator's household who provide their own mobile number directly to the operator and give express consent (verbal and/or written) to receive these messages. Opt-in is confirmed by a one-time welcome message, and the operator keeps a dated record of each recipient's consent. By opting in, you agree to receive recurring text messages as described below.

3. Messages you will receive

  • Notifications about your recent transactions and remaining budget.
  • Replies to questions you send (for example, "can I afford $90?").

4. Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies based on your account activity. Message and data rates may apply. Your mobile carrier's rates and terms govern any charges.

5. How to opt out

Reply STOP at any time to stop all messages. You will receive one confirmation message, after which no further messages are sent unless you reply START to opt back in.
