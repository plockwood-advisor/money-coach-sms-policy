Money Coach

Automated spending alerts by text, generated from your own linked account activity.
Privacy Policy · Terms & Conditions

Money Coach is an automated text-notification service. After each card transaction it sends an automatic alert showing your remaining budget, and it replies automatically when you text a question about your balance or budget (for example, "can I afford $90?"). All messages are generated automatically by software from your own linked account data. It sells nothing, sends no marketing, and is not a lender, debt collector, credit-repair service, or financial institution.

What you'll receive

  • Notifications about your recent transactions and remaining budget.
  • Replies to questions you text in.
