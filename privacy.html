Money Coach — SMS Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2, 2026

Money Coach ("the Service", "we", "us") is an automated text-notification service. Enrolled users receive application-generated SMS alerts about their own account activity. The Service sells nothing and sends no marketing. This policy explains what information we collect over SMS and how we handle it.

Information we collect

  • Mobile phone number and the content of messages exchanged with the Service, so we can send notifications and reply to questions.
  • Bank account transaction and balance information, accessed with the account holder's explicit authorization through a third-party financial-data provider (Plaid), used only to generate spending notifications and answer the recipient's own questions about their balance.

We do not collect this information from anyone who has not personally consented, and we do not purchase, rent, or otherwise acquire phone numbers from third parties.

How we use information

  • Send short, factual notifications about recent transactions and remaining budget.
  • Answer the recipient's own questions about their balance and whether a purchase fits their budget.
  • Operate, maintain, and secure the Service.

SMS program terms

  • Recipients opt in through the consent form on our home page (or by providing their mobile number directly to the operator) and by giving express consent to receive messages.
  • Message frequency varies with account activity. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply HELP for help, or STOP to unsubscribe at any time. After STOP, no further messages are sent unless the recipient replies START to opt back in.
SMS consent and mobile data. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Service providers

We use a small number of third-party providers strictly to operate the Service, never for marketing. They process data only as needed to perform their function and are bound by their own privacy and security terms:

  • Plaid — secure connection to the recipient's bank for transaction and balance data.
  • Twilio — delivery of the SMS messages themselves.
  • Anthropic — formats the wording of outgoing messages. All money calculations are performed by our own software, not by any AI provider.

Data storage, security, and retention

Data is stored privately and access is limited to the individual who operates the Service. Bank-access credentials are encrypted at rest. We retain information only as long as needed to run the Service, and delete it on request.

Your choices

  • Stop messages: reply STOP at any time.
  • Access or deletion: contact us (below) to review or delete your information.

Children

The Service is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.
